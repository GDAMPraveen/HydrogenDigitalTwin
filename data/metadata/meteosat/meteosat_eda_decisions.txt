
METEOSAT — EDA AND PREPROCESSING DECISIONS

Dataset:
Bengaluru, 2017
Observations: 35,040
Sampling interval: 15 minutes

MISSING VALUES:
- No missing values identified.
- No imputation required.

DATA INTEGRITY:
- No duplicate observations.
- Chronological ordering verified.
- Complete 15-minute timestamp sequence.
- Original observations preserved.

SENSOR QUALITY:
- Nighttime zero irradiance values retained.
- No negative irradiance values.
- IQR outliers retained.
- No automatic sensor-fault classification.

FEATURE AND TARGET SUITABILITY:
- GHI: potential solar irradiance target.
- DNI and DHI: candidate solar variables.
- Temperature, wind speed, humidity and pressure:
  candidate meteorological predictors.
- Solar Zenith Angle: solar geometry predictor.
- Month and Hour: temporal feature candidates.
- Timestamp: temporal ordering and alignment only.
- Estimated_GHI and GHI_Residual: diagnostic-only variables.

MODELLING:
- No feature scaling performed.
- No observations removed.
- Forecasting must respect information available at
  the forecast origin.
- Contemporaneous DNI/DHI must not be used carelessly
  to predict GHI because of their physical relationship.
- Chronological 70/15/15 split is a preliminary proposal.

INTEGRATION:
- Dataset does not directly measure hydrogen production
  or renewable electricity generation.
- Cross-year integration with NLR requires justification.
