
METEOSAT — VISUAL EDA FINDINGS

1. SOLAR IRRADIANCE TIME SERIES
- Repeating daily solar cycle observed.
- GHI, DNI and DHI fall to zero at night.
- Seasonal variation is visible.
- A substantial irradiance reduction occurs around
  January 26–27.
- No missing timestamps were identified in the full dataset.
- February 10 had only one observation in the inspected
  January 20–February 10 interval.

2. MONTHLY SOLAR TRENDS
- GHI is relatively high from February to April.
- DNI reaches its highest monthly mean in February.
- DNI declines substantially during July and August.
- DHI increases during June–September.
- Pattern is consistent with seasonal changes in
  direct and diffuse solar conditions.

3. IRRADIANCE DISTRIBUTIONS
- Large zero-value concentrations correspond to nighttime.
- GHI and DHI zero percentage: 50.38%.
- DNI zero percentage: 57.64%.
- Irradiance distributions are zero-inflated and
  non-normal overall.
- Nighttime zeros are valid observations.

4. METEOROLOGICAL DISTRIBUTIONS
- Temperature is approximately bell-shaped with
  some right skew.
- Wind speed is positively skewed.
- Relative humidity has a concentration at 100%.
- Pressure has a discrete, narrow distribution.

5. SENSOR CORRELATION HEATMAP
- GHI-DNI: approximately +0.845.
- GHI-DHI: approximately +0.758.
- GHI-Temperature: approximately +0.702.
- GHI-Solar Zenith Angle: approximately -0.817.
- Temperature-Relative Humidity: approximately -0.790.
- Feature redundancy and target leakage must be considered.

6. DAYTIME VS NIGHTTIME
- Nighttime irradiance values are zero.
- Daytime mean temperature: 26.62 degrees C.
- Nighttime mean temperature: 20.17 degrees C.
- Daytime mean wind speed: 3.17 m/s.
- Nighttime mean wind speed: 2.11 m/s.
- Daytime median relative humidity: 59.92%.
- Nighttime median relative humidity: 95.36%.

INTERPRETATION:
These are observational findings. They do not independently
establish causal mechanisms or prove sensor faults.
